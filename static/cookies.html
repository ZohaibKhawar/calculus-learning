Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This page lists everything CalcLearners stores in your browser and why. It goes with our Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions.

The short version

We set one cookie of our own, and it only keeps you signed in. Google's sign-in button adds one more. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies, and we don't track you across other websites. A few preferences are saved in your browser so the site remembers them.

1. What we store

A cookie is a small piece of text a website asks your browser to keep and send back on later visits. Local storage is similar, but it stays in your browser and is not sent to our server.

NameKindWhat it's forHow long
cl_id Cookie (essential) Keeps you signed in. It holds your account's ID in a signed form that page scripts can't read, and it is only sent over HTTPS. Up to 400 days after your last visit. Cleared when you sign out.
g_state Cookie (Google sign-in) Set by Google's sign-in button on the sign-in screen, to remember the state of its sign-in prompt. Managed by Google.
theme Local storage Remembers whether you chose light or dark mode. Until you clear your browser's site data.
style Local storage Remembers the learning style you picked (watch, read or practice). Until you clear your browser's site data.
vp-rate, vp-voice, vp-cc Local storage Remember your video player settings: speed, voiceover and captions. Until you clear your browser's site data.
uid Local storage Left over from an older version of the site that had no accounts. It is cleared the next time you sign in. Until you sign in.

2. Other services on our pages

Some parts of the site come from other companies. They may store their own cookies or similar data, under their own policies:

  • Google sign-in is loaded on the sign-in screen. As well as the g_state cookie listed above, Google may set cookies on its own domains.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile is the bot check on the create-account form. Cloudflare may store information in your browser while the check runs.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver fonts and the math and animation libraries. They don't set cookies for us, but they do see your IP address when your browser downloads those files.

3. Why there is no cookie banner

Everything in the table above is either needed for the site and its sign-in to work or is a preference you set yourself. We don't use cookies that need a separate opt-in, such as advertising or analytics cookies. If that ever changes, we will update this page and ask you first.

4. Your choices

  • Signing out clears the sign-in cookie.
  • You can delete cookies and site data in your browser's settings at any time. Your saved preferences will go back to their defaults.
  • You can block cookies in your browser, but then you won't be able to sign in.

5. Changes and contact

If we change what we store, we will update the table and the date at the top. Questions go to CalcLearners@gmail.com, or through the Send feedback page if you have an account.
