Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This page lists everything CalcLearners stores in your browser and why. It goes with our Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions.

The short version

We set one cookie. It holds a random ID so the site can save your progress without an account, and it keeps you signed in if you have one. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies, and we don't track you across other websites. A few preferences are saved in your browser so the site remembers them.

1. What we store

A cookie is a small piece of text a website asks your browser to keep and send back on later visits. Local storage is similar, but it stays in your browser and is not sent to our server. Session storage is the same again, except that it is cleared when you close the tab.

NameKindWhat it's forHow long
cl_id Cookie (essential) Holds your visitor ID, so your progress, notes and posts are saved for this browser. While you are signed in to an account it holds the account's ID instead, which is what keeps you signed in. flash Session storage Holds a short message, such as "Signed out", across the page reload that follows signing in or out. Until the page has shown it, a moment later.
uid Local storage Left over from the first version of the site, which kept the visitor ID here. It is cleared the next time you visit. Until your next visit.

2. Other services on our pages

Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver the site's fonts and its math and animation libraries. They don't set cookies for us, but they do see your IP address when your browser downloads those files.

3. Why there is no cookie banner

Everything in the table above is either needed for the site to work or is there because of something you did yourself, such as a preference you set. We don't use cookies that need a separate opt-in, such as advertising or analytics cookies. If that ever changes, we will update this page and ask you first.

4. Your choices

  • "Delete my data" on the My progress page deletes what is saved for your browser and gives it a fresh visitor ID. With an account, "Delete my account" on your profile page does the same for the account.
  • Signing out takes your account out of the cookie. The account itself stays until you delete it.
  • You can delete cookies and site data in your browser's settings at any time. The site will then treat you as a new visitor: your saved progress is no longer connected to your browser, and your preferences go back to their defaults.
  • You can block cookies in your browser, but then the site can't save your progress and may stop working.

5. Changes and contact

If we change what we store, we will update the table and the date at the top. Questions go to CalcLearners@gmail.com, or through the Send feedback page.
